# 🚀 Avinya – Entrepreneurship Redefined 💡  

🌟 **Avinya** is a **flagship entrepreneurship event** hosted by the **Entrepreneurship Cell (E-Cell)** at **Don Bosco College of Engineering, Fatorda**. 
  It brings together **schools, higher secondaries, and colleges across Goa**, fostering **innovation, business acumen, and startup culture**.  

🌍 This website was built to showcase **event details, registrations, and updates**, providing a seamless experience for participants and organizers. 🖥️📢  

🔥 Empowering the next generation of entrepreneurs!
